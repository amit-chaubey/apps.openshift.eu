# chatwoot 0.4.0

## Changes

### New value `odoo.enabled`: Odoo tickets and leads in Chatwoot

With `odoo.enabled`, Deployment `chatwoot-odoo-bridge` mirrors Odoo helpdesk tickets and
CRM leads into the API inboxes `odoo.ticketInbox` and `odoo.leadInbox`, one conversation
per record, with each customer mail as an incoming message. A public staff reply is posted
on the Odoo record, and Odoo mails it to the customer. Resolving a conversation moves the
ticket to `odoo.solvedStage`; closing the ticket resolves the conversation. Odoo posts
signed events to `https://<route.hostname>/odoo/events`; the bridge reads the records
through the Odoo JSON-2 API with `secrets.odooApiKey`.

A staff reply that Odoo does not accept is retried by the bridge for about 40 minutes,
then reported to staff in a private note; a reply the bridge could not deliver before its
pod stopped gets the same note. New values `monitoring.enabled` and
`monitoring.alerts` add a ServiceMonitor and alert rules for the bridge.

New Secrets `chatwoot-odoo` and `chatwoot-odoo-bridge`, and with `secrets.type`
`externalSecret` the vault items `chatwoot-odoo-api-key`, `chatwoot-odoo-event-secret`,
`chatwoot-odoo-bridge-token` and `chatwoot-odoo-inbox-secret`.

## Upgrade

Nothing to do while `odoo.enabled` stays false: the render is unchanged apart from the
chart label. To switch it on, follow the Odoo bridge steps under Delivery instructions in
the README; the prepare Job then creates the bridge user, the two inboxes and the custom
attributes.
