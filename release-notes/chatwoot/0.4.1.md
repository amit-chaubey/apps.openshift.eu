# chatwoot 0.4.1

## Changes

### Odoo bridge: mailed-in leads and a stop without lost replies

- A lead that Odoo created from an incoming mail keeps the whole From header
  (`"Name" <address>`). The bridge now gives Chatwoot and the reply the address alone;
  before, Chatwoot refused the contact and the lead never reached Chatwoot.
- When the bridge pod stops, replies it has not delivered get a private note within its
  shutdown budget of 15 seconds, also while Odoo answers slowly. Queued Odoo events are
  not run at a stop; Odoo sends them again.
- Alert ChatwootOdooBridgeEventsFailing describes that it counts the failed deliveries of
  one event or several.
