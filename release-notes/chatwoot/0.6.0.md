# chatwoot 0.6.0

## Changes

### AI triage sorts each new conversation

With `triage.enabled` the agent sorts each new conversation of `draftAgent.inboxes` into one
of `triage.categories` before the first draft. The bridge gives the conversation to the
category's team, adds a private note with the category and a summary, and labels the
conversation with the category and `triage.label`. A conversation that already has a team
or an assignee keeps it; an answer without a known category, or no answer, takes
`triage.fallback`. The prepare Job creates the labels and teams, with auto assignment, adds
the emails of `triage.teams` that have a user to their team and to the draft inboxes, and
switches the draft inboxes' own auto assignment off, so the triage's team decides. Off by
default.

### Tenant facts in the triage note

With `tenantFacts.enabled` the triage note lists facts about the customer's own tenant from
the Kubernetes API: pods that are not ready, CronJob successes, volume claims that are not
bound, recent warning events and Velero backups. The tenant comes from the contact's email
through a namespace annotation, never from the message. The cluster administrator binds a
ClusterRole to ServiceAccount `chatwoot-agent-bridge` (see the README). Off by default.

### The bridge catches up on missed messages

Every `draftAgent.sweepSeconds` (default 60) the agent bridge looks at the open
conversations of the draft inboxes changed in the last `draftAgent.sweepMaxAgeHours`
(default 24) and drafts for each customer message that waits without a draft, for example
one that arrived while the bridge restarted. After a failure it waits longer each time, at
most an hour.

## Upgrade

Nothing to do. Every pod that reads the scripts ConfigMap restarts once: Chatwoot web,
Sidekiq, the login gate (with SSO), the Odoo bridge and the agent bridge. With
`draftAgent.enabled` the agent bridge then drafts the customer messages of the last 24
hours that wait without a draft. Set `draftAgent.sweepSeconds: 0` to keep the earlier
behaviour.
