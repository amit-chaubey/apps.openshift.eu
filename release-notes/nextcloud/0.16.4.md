## Changes

### Icons and arrows for mail, the office agent and TURN

The Mailu components show their product icons and arrows between them in the topology view of the OpenShift console, the office agent shows the ONLYOFFICE icon and the TURN server the coturn icon.

Nothing to do. Nextcloud and the Mailu front restart once, as on every chart version; the other workloads only get new metadata.
