# nextcloud 0.17.3

Released 2026-11-04.

## Changes

### The platform's single sign-on Secret is named after its client

Nextcloud and webmail read Secret `public-nextcloud` instead of `nextcloud-sso`: the platform
names the Secret after the Keycloak client, `<realm>-<Deployment>`.

## Upgrade

A platform that still writes `nextcloud-sso` must write `public-nextcloud`. Until it does,
`sso.enabled` finds no Secret and Nextcloud removes its provider.
